Mouse & Turtle Adventure

Join Mickey and Bowser on their quest for the Golden Cup of Infinity!

How to Play:

Mickey Controls: WASD (W=Jump, A/D=Move, S=Throw Paintball)

Bowser Controls: Arrow Keys (Up=Jump, Left/Right=Move, Down=Fire Attack)

Goal: Work together to reach portals and find the Golden Cup!

Mickey Health: 100
Bowser Health: 100
Level: 1
Level Navigation:
Mickey Controls: WASD (W=Jump, A/D=Move, S=Throw Paintball)
Bowser Controls: Arrow Keys (Up=Jump, Left/Right=Move, Down=Fire Attack)
